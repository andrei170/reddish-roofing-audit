What we do

Whole roofs, small repairs, and everything between.

Most of what we do is slate and clay tile on Manchester housing stock — the sort of roofs that were laid eighty years ago and are only now giving up. We will tell you honestly whether yours needs replacing or just fixing.

New Roofs

New roof installation

Full strip and re-roof. Timbers checked, new membrane and battens, slate or tile relaid, dry ridge and verge, all leadwork renewed.

  • Scaffold arranged and included
  • Dry fixed ridge and verge as standard
  • Photographs sent as the job goes on
Repairs

Roof repairs & emergencies

Slipped tiles, cracked slates, storm damage, leaks. We make it watertight first and talk about the permanent fix afterwards.

  • 24/7 emergency call-outs
  • Photo evidence for insurers
  • Honest answer on repair versus replace
Roofline

Guttering, fascias & chimneys

A roof is only as good as its drainage. Gutters, downpipes, fascias and soffits, plus chimney repairs and repointing.

  • Flat roofing and repairs

Paying for it

Nobody budgets for a roof.

It fails when it fails, usually in the middle of winter, and usually when there is nothing put aside for it. So you do not have to find it all at once — finance is available on larger jobs.

Ask when we quote and we will show you the monthly figure next to the total, so you can decide with both numbers in front of you rather than just the big one.

Finance is provided by an authorised lender and is subject to status and affordability checks. Full lender details and a representative example are shown before any application. Concept note: the live page must carry the lender's own FCA wording and representative example verbatim.
